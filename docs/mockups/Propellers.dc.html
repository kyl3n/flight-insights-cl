Bench-test a 23×6.9 on the same motor, or fit one set and fly a steady 60 s hover at 28.0 kg with ESC telemetry logging; the app grades the prediction from that flight. It rests on this aircraft's measured hover and the family's tables, and is advised only because its gain is more than twice the validated error. The app never changes the aircraft.

  1. Fit the propellers, each turning the way its motor does, balanced; check every tip clears its neighbours and the frame.
  2. Props off: motor test for direction; the notch's lowest frequency below the new hover speed (45 Hz).
  3. Calm air: hover 60 s hands-off at 28.0 kg with ESC telemetry, then a short normal flight.
  4. Land and refit the old propellers if it oscillates, runs hot, warns of thrust loss, or needs more than about 70% throttle to hover.
