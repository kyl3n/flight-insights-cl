GNSS lost

The aircraft willhold on dead reckoning, then land in place (EKF failsafe)
Holds its estimate forabout 40 s, measured
Travels on dead reckoningabout 150 m before it is 10 m off

Operator

  1. Switch to AltHold.
  2. Turn the nose to the home heading.
  3. Fly home on the heading at walking pace; keep it in sight.
  4. Land by sight; do not wait for GNSS to return.

Radio link lost

The aircraft willafter 5 s, return to launch (GCS failsafe, RTL)
Joystick commandsstop holding after 3 s
Link usually returnswithin 2.1 km of the station

Operator

  1. Do nothing for 5 s: let the failsafe start the return.
  2. Walk the ground station clear of obstacles toward the aircraft.
  3. When the link returns, take over in Loiter.

Link and GNSS both lost

The aircraft willland where it is: RTL needs a position
It cannotreturn to take-off with today's settings and sensors

Operator

  1. Note the time and last seen direction.
  2. Keep the ground station running to catch the link if it returns.
  3. Search along the last heading from the last known position.

This is the fleet goal HL-07 does not meet yet: see Navigation and GNSS, "What would get it home".

Motor or ESC lost

The aircraft willhold height and yaw on the other 7 at or below 30.5 kg
Above 30.5 kgthe busiest motor may saturate

Operator

  1. Stop any manoeuvre; let it level.
  2. Descend at a steady rate.
  3. Land at the nearest clear spot, not at home.

Suspected spoofing: position jumps or drifts while GNSS looks healthy

  1. Reject GNSS first with the GNSS-disable switch: the estimator may still trust it. Not set up on HL-07 yet: it is in the plan.
  2. Then follow "GNSS lost".
