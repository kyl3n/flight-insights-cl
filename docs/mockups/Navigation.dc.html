GNSS events

Classed as jamming, spoofing, obstruction, onboard interference or receiver fault
  1. ■Jamminghigh confidence · both receivers12:20 – 14:05

    AGC fell 32% and C/N0 14 dB across every constellation together; the CW indicator rose; satellites 21 → 0 and the fix was lost for 41 s. The position stayed consistent until it was lost. HL-03 saw the same at the same time: an outside source.

  2. ▲Onboard interferencemedium confidence · GPS 1 only3:05 – 3:40

    The CW indicator rose 40% each time the video transmitter keyed up, with C/N0 down 4 dB on GPS 1 alone. Move the video antenna away from GPS 1, or shield the transmitter.

What would get it home

Researched mitigations, cheapest first where they help equally
  1. 1Calibrate drag fusion, then turn on the dead-reckoning failsafesettings and one calibration flight

    For the goal: the estimator knows the wind when GNSS goes, so dead reckoning drifts less; FS_DR_TIMEOUT is then set from the hold measured on a drill. Cheapest first step; not enough on its own for a 2 km return.

    No hardware · procedure ready in the planArduPilot: wind estimation; dead-reckoning failsafe
  2. 2Optical flow with a radar or lidar rangefinderhardware

    For the goal: velocity over the ground without GNSS, so the aircraft can hold and fly home on its own heading. Works over textured ground within the sensor's height range; check it covers your 10–15 m working height and the terrain you fly over.

    Sensor and mount · second EKF source set and a switch or Lua scriptArduPilot: optical flow; GPS to non-GPS transitions
  3. 3Visual odometry (VIO)hardware · planned

    For the goal: camera-based velocity and position from a companion computer, fed as external navigation; less dependent on height and texture than flow.

    Companion computer and camera · EK3_SRC2 from external navigationArduPilot: non-GPS navigation, visual odometry
  4. 4SLAM or map-based visual navigationhardware · research

    For the goal: an absolute position without GNSS, so drift does not grow with distance; the only option here that bounds error over long returns.

    Most compute and integration workArduPilot: external navigation (ODOMETRY)
  5. 5Harden GNSS itselfhardware

    For the goal: loses GNSS less often: a second receiver on another band or make, antenna placement and ground plane, a controlled-pattern (CRPA) anti-jam antenna. Does not help once jamming is strong enough.

    Receiver or antennaArduPilot: GPS blending; u-blox jamming indicator

Ranked by measured benefit for the goal on this airframe; re-ranked as drills and outages are flown. A Lua source-switching script can be generated for review, never loaded automatically.
