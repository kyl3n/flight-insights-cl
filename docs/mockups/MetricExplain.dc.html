ESC temperature, peak

esc.temp.peak · °C
96 °C ▲ Caution ESC 3 · HL-07 · flight 142

What it is

The hottest reading from any of the aircraft's ESCs while it was armed. ESCs heat up with the current they switch. Past a set temperature they reduce power to protect themselves, which on a multirotor means less thrust on that arm.

How it is measured

Source
ESC.Temp, every ESC instance
Sensor
The ESC's own, sent over DShot or serial telemetry
Calculation
Highest value across all ESCs while armed

Boundaries: this build's ESC protection setting (100 °C) and the 15 °C margin your team set for this platform; ESC behaviour from the BLHeli_32 manual.

This value

  • 4 °C below the protection setting.
  • 14 °C above the median of the other seven ESCs.
  • This airframe usually peaks at 78–82 °C.
  • Rising for 6 flights, since flight 137.

What moves it

Load (payload, wind, hard manoeuvres), airflow over the ESC, outside temperature, a worn motor or damaged prop, and noise reaching the motors when filters let it through.

Related, and not to be confused with

Motor temperature is a separate field, ESC.MotTemp, with its own limits from the motor maker.
